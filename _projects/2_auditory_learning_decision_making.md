Auditory decision-making critically depends on structural connections between superior temporal cortex and dorsal
striatum. These auditory corticostriatal connections have been mapped in animal models — including non-human
primates, where primary auditory cortex preferentially connects to putamen while caudate head receives most of its
input from anterior superior temporal cortex — but they remain poorly characterized in humans.

Using ultra-high field 7T MRI, we acquired sub-2mm isotropic resolution BOLD fMRI from participants categorizing
speech stimuli on the basis of dynamically varying pitch patterns. With univariate and representational similarity
analyses, dorsal striatum and auditory cortex are both involved in auditory category learning from the earliest
learning stages, suggesting distinct functional roles for striatal subdivisions in auditory speech categorization.
