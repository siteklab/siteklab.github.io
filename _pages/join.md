## Available positions

The lab opens in **January 2027** in the [Department of Speech, Language, and Hearing](https://bbs.utdallas.edu/departments/speech-language-and-hearing/) in the School of Behavioral and Brain Sciences
at UT Dallas, and we are recruiting at every level. We study how the brain supports human communication,
with a focus on the subcortical auditory system and speech processing,
using fMRI, diffusion MRI tractography, ultra-high field (7T) imaging, MEG, and EEG,
along with audiometric and behavioral assessments.

You'll fit in well if you're curious about auditory and speech neuroscience and comfortable with (or excited to learn) quantitative methods.
Programming experience (Python, shell, or similar) and familiarity with neuroimaging analysis are a plus, but not required for every role.

**A note on email:** I read every email myself, and I'm much more likely to respond to one you wrote in your own words.
Tell me briefly who you
are, what draws you to the lab's work, and which position you're interested in.
Email [kevin.sitek@utdallas.edu](mailto:kevin.sitek@utdallas.edu).

## Postdoctoral researchers

We welcome applications from people with a PhD (or who are close to finishing one) in neuroscience, speech and hearing science, psychology,
biomedical engineering, physics, computer science, or a related field.
Experience with neuroimaging acquisition or analysis and/or advanced computational techniques is especially valuable.

The start date is negotiable. Funding is currently available through university startup funds as well as NIH grant funds.
You would also be encouraged and supported to apply for your own funding through federal and foundation fellowship and grant mechanisms,
although this would be dependent on your own career goals and eligibility.

To apply, email a CV, a short statement (about one page) of your research interests and how they connect to the lab,
and contact information for 2–3 references.

Postdoctoral candidates should also consider applying to the [Callier Center Postdoctoral Program](https://calliercenter.utdallas.edu/research/callier-postdoctoral-program/).

## PhD students

PhD students join the lab through the UT Dallas
[Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences PhD program](https://bbs.utdallas.edu/departments/speech-language-and-hearing/graduate-programs/speech-language-and-hearing-sciences-phd/).
Students in the program are fully funded for up to five years. Applications are due **November 15** for admission the following fall.

If you're thinking about applying, please email me first with your CV and a few sentences about your research interests. I'm happy to talk
about whether the lab is a good fit.

The lab is currently accepting students for Fall 2027 and beyond, so reach out anytime!

## Undergraduates

UT Dallas undergraduates can join the lab as volunteers or for research credit.
Students from cognitive science, psychology, neuroscience, speech-language-hearing sciences, computer science, and engineering are all welcome. No prior research experience is needed.

To get involved, email a CV or résumé, an unofficial transcript, and a short note about your interests and how many hours per week you're
available.

## Working at UT Dallas

The lab is part of the [School of Behavioral and Brain Sciences](https://bbs.utdallas.edu/) and is affiliated with the
[Callier Center for Communication Disorders](https://calliercenter.utdallas.edu/),
which has research and clinical facilities on the Richardson campus and in Dallas, next to UT Southwestern Medical Center.

At the **Callier Center Dallas**, lab members have access to:

- our lab's EEG, audiometry, and behavioral testing facilities
- clinical research experts and collaborators across speech, language, hearing, voice, balance, and swallowing

Nearby in Dallas, the [Center for Brain Health](https://brainhealth.utdallas.edu/) and the [Center for Vital Longevity](https://vitallongevity.utdallas.edu/) have multiple 3T MRI scanners and additional EEG systems, along with a rich community of neuroscientists studying the human brain and behavior.

Through collaborations with **UT Southwestern**, lab members have access to:

- the **7T MRI** at the [Advanced Imaging Research Center (AIRC)](https://www.utsouthwestern.edu/departments/airc/research/ultra-high-field-mri.html),
  one of the most powerful human MRI scanners available
- the [Magnetoencephalography (MEG) Core](https://www.utsouthwestern.edu/research/core-facilities/meg-core/), home to the only MEG scanner in
  Dallas

In addition to internal lab computing resources, lab members have access to multiple [high performance computing clusters through UT Dallas](https://hpc.utdallas.edu/systems/):

- the Juno and Ganymede clusters
- the [Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC)](https://tacc.utexas.edu/)

## Living in Dallas

The Dallas–Fort Worth area is one of the largest metro areas in the country, with a relatively low cost of living, a huge food scene, great museums, good music, and excellent professional sports.
DFW is a major airport hub with easy travel almost anywhere in the world.
