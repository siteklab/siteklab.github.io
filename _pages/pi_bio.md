Kevin Sitek is a neuroscientist studying the brain regions and connections that underlie human perception and
communication, with a particular focus on subcortical auditory structures, speech–motor integration, and auditory
learning. He received his PhD in 2019 from Harvard University's Program in Speech and Hearing Bioscience and
Technology, mentored by [Dr. Satra Ghosh](http://satra.cogitatum.org/) at the McGovern Institute for Brain Research
at MIT, and completed postdoctoral training at Baylor College of Medicine and the University of Pittsburgh before
joining Northwestern University as a research assistant professor. He joins UT Dallas as a tenure-track assistant
professor in January 2027.
