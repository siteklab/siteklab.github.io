Kevin Sitek, Ph.D., is an Assistant Professor (starting January 2027) in the Department of Speech, Language, and Hearing in the School of Behavioral and Brain Sciences at the University of Texas at Dallas.
He is a neuroscientist studying the brain regions and connections that underlie human perception and
communication, with a particular focus on subcortical auditory structures, speech–motor integration, and auditory
learning.

Dr. Sitek received his Bachelor of Arts in Linguistics and Cognitive Science at UC Berkeley, where he contributed to research in the Phonology Laboratory. 
He then worked as a staff research assistant at the San Francisco VA Medical Center/UCSF in the Brain Imaging and EEG Laboratory.

He received his PhD from the Harvard University Program in Speech and Hearing Bioscience and Technology, working at the MIT McGovern Institute for Brain Research. After a postdoctoral appointment at the Baylor College of Medicine Department of Neuroscience, he joined the University of Pittsburgh as a research scientist.

Immediately prior to joining UT Dallas, Dr. Sitek was a research assistant professor in Communication Sciences and Disorders at Northwestern University.
